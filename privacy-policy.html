Privacy Policy

How the Law Office of Christopher M. Stahl, LLC, handles the information you share through this website.

The Law Office of Christopher M. Stahl, LLC (“the Firm,” “we,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information you choose to provide. When you complete a contact or consultation form, you may submit your name, email address, telephone number, and a brief description of your legal matter. Our web servers and analytics tools may also automatically record limited technical data, such as your browser type, device, and general location, in the ordinary course of operating the site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide for one purpose: to respond to your inquiry. That may include returning your call or email, answering general questions, and determining whether the Firm is able to assist you. We do not use form submissions to send unsolicited marketing, and we keep your information no longer than necessary for these purposes.

Confidentiality & No Attorney-Client Relationship

Submitting information through this website does not create an attorney-client relationship and does not, by itself, make your communication privileged or confidential. An attorney-client relationship is formed only after we have checked for conflicts and both you and the Firm have agreed to representation in writing. Please do not send sensitive or time-critical details through the website until a relationship has been established.

Cookies & Analytics

This site may use cookies and basic analytics to understand how visitors find and use our pages so that we can improve them. This information is aggregated and is not used to personally identify you. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, although some parts of the site may not function as intended if you do.

Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may rely on trusted service providers—such as our website host or email and analytics services—to operate the site, and they may process limited data only as needed to provide those services. We may also disclose information where required by law.

Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for adults seeking legal services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us so that we can remove it.

How to Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to ask about the information we hold, please call 318-746-5610 or write to us at 707 Benton Rd, Ste 200, Bossier City, LA 71111.

This page is a general template provided for convenience and should be reviewed by the Firm’s attorney before publication to confirm it reflects current practices and applicable law.
