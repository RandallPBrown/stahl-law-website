Family Law Attorney in Bossier City & Shreveport

Compassionate, practical representation for custody, divorce, and property matters across Northwest Louisiana.

Making a decision that affects your family can be life-altering. The choice to pursue custody, divorce, emancipation, or separation of property is rarely easy, given the emotional demands involved. Chris Stahl helps guide you through what is often a heartbreaking situation by tailoring a plan to your needs and explaining the challenges and pitfalls of your particular circumstances. The firm also handles adoptions.

Child Custody

Custody and support are usually addressed within the larger divorce case but may be revisited as circumstances change. Chris works to protect your relationship with your children and to reach arrangements that serve their best interests under Louisiana law.

Divorce

Whether your divorce is amicable or contested, Chris helps you understand your options — including how related matters like custody, support, and the division of property are typically resolved as part of the larger case.

Adoption

Adoption is a joyful step, but the legal process can be detailed. Chris guides families through the requirements so you can focus on welcoming your child.

Emancipation

Emancipation matters require careful attention to Louisiana’s specific legal standards. Chris can advise on whether emancipation is appropriate and help you navigate the process.

Division of Community Property

Louisiana’s community-property rules can make dividing assets and debts complex. Chris helps ensure property is identified, valued, and divided fairly across the Shreveport–Bossier City area.
