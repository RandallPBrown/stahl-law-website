Why Estate Planning Matters

Many people put off estate planning because they assume it is only for the wealthy or the elderly. In reality, nearly everyone benefits from having a plan in place. A solid estate plan ensures your assets are distributed according to your wishes, that your loved ones are provided for, and that someone you trust can make medical and financial decisions on your behalf if you are ever unable to do so yourself. Without a plan, those decisions are left to the courts and to Louisiana’s default laws — not to you.

Wills and Trusts

A will is a legal document that directs how your assets should be distributed after you pass away and can name guardians for minor children. Chris can help you create a will that reflects your wishes and ensures your loved ones are cared for. He can also help you establish a trust, which can provide added protection for your assets and offer benefits such as probate avoidance and potential tax savings. A revocable living trust, for example, lets you transfer assets into the trust during your lifetime and distribute them privately and efficiently to your beneficiaries upon your death.

Powers of Attorney

A power of attorney is a legal document that lets you appoint someone to make financial and/or medical decisions on your behalf if you become unable to do so. Chris can help you put a power of attorney in place that reflects your wishes and ensures someone you trust is ready to manage your affairs should the need arise. This is one of the most important — and most overlooked — pieces of a complete estate plan.

Healthcare Directives

A living will, or advance healthcare directive, lets you state your wishes about medical treatment and end-of-life care in advance. Pairing a healthcare directive with a medical power of attorney ensures that your preferences are known and that a trusted person can speak for you if you cannot speak for yourself. These documents spare your family difficult guesswork during an already stressful time.

A Personalized Approach — and Louisiana-Specific Guidance

Chris takes a personalized approach to estate planning, recognizing that every client’s situation is unique. He begins by getting to know each client and their individual needs and goals, then builds a customized plan that may include a will, trust, power of attorney, or other documents. Louisiana’s laws have features you will not find elsewhere — including forced heirship, which entitles certain heirs to a portion of your estate regardless of your wishes. Although Louisiana has no state estate tax, larger estates may still face federal estate tax. Chris helps you navigate these rules and keeps your plan current as your life changes, whether through marriage, children, or new assets.
