Criminal Defense Attorney in Bossier City & Shreveport

Experienced defense for misdemeanor and felony charges throughout North Louisiana.

If you’ve been charged with a crime in the Bossier City or Shreveport area, you’re probably wondering what happens next. Will you go to prison? What will happen to your family? No one expects to be charged with a crime, and when it happens people often feel overwhelmed. The first thing you should do is hire an experienced criminal defense attorney like Christopher Stahl, who has handled hundreds of misdemeanor and felony cases in North Louisiana — including drug and alcohol offenses and theft.

Do I Need a Criminal Defense Attorney?

The short answer is yes. A criminal defense attorney guides you through the courtroom process. You may believe the police have no evidence, that they’ve made a mistake, and that simply explaining yourself will resolve everything — but courts don’t always work that way. The law is complex and the rules of the courtroom can be confusing, so it’s best to have an experienced attorney represent you. Your and your family’s reputations may also be at stake, and Chris can help you mitigate the fallout of a charge.

What Does a Criminal Defense Attorney Do?

A defense attorney’s job doesn’t begin or end in the courtroom. It is your attorney’s job to show reasonable doubt as to your guilt — and short of that, to argue you deserve a lighter sentence than the prosecutor recommends. Your attorney is your advocate throughout the entire process.

Your Consultation

First, Chris meets with you to discuss the facts of your case. It’s important to share as much information as possible and to be completely honest. This meeting takes place before you officially hire the attorney; afterward, Chris can estimate your potential penalties, explain how he can help, and you can decide whether to move forward.

Before the Trial

Much of the work happens long before trial — reviewing the prosecutor’s information, conducting interviews, researching similar cases, and building your defense. In many cases an attorney can keep the matter from going to trial at all by negotiating a plea bargain or seeking a dismissal. A plea deal is a negotiated compromise: in exchange for a guilty plea, you receive a lighter sentence. Skill and experience matter here. A plea isn’t right for every defendant, and when the prosecution believes it has enough evidence, the case may go to trial.

The Trial

If your case goes to trial, your attorney helps select the jury, calls witnesses, and questions the prosecution’s witnesses on your behalf — fighting for the best possible outcome.

What Is Attorney–Client Privilege?

Many defendants wonder whether they can be honest with their attorney. In all cases, it is essential to be honest. Attorney-client privilege is the law’s version of doctor-patient confidentiality: what you discuss stays between the two of you. There are very few exceptions, and in the vast majority of cases — even felony cases — there is no need to break privilege, so you can speak openly without fear.

What About Attorney Fees?

Defense attorneys charge in different ways and usually require a retainer up front:

  • By the case: a fixed amount for a given charge. If the matter resolves quickly the fee stays the same — and if it takes more work, your cost doesn’t go up either.
  • By the hour: you receive periodic updates on time spent. A few hours means lighter costs; a case demanding significant time costs more.

If you cannot afford an attorney, the Constitution guarantees you a court-appointed one. Those attorneys are often capable, but their caseloads are heavy. Because your freedom is at stake, hiring a private attorney like Chris allows focused time and energy on your case. Fees and payment are discussed during your consultation.

First, look at experience: Chris has handled hundreds of criminal matters in Louisiana, and a local attorney develops relationships that can help in negotiation. Second, and most importantly, meet with the attorney, be honest about your situation, and decide whether this is who you want by your side.
