Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about fees, court costs, expungements, and family law from a Bossier City attorney who has helped Northwest Louisiana clients since day one.

Common Questions

What Clients Ask Most

If you don’t see your question below, call the office at 318-746-5610 and we’ll walk you through it.

Do you charge a consultation fee?

Consultations for personal injury, criminal defense, and expungements are free. Family law, successions, and civil disputes require a $350 consultation fee, paid up front.

How much will my case cost?

Fees depend on the matter; criminal cases are often a flat per-case fee, while others may be hourly or flat. Chris explains expected costs at your consultation.

What are court costs?

Court costs are fees charged by the court itself (filing, service of process, etc.), separate from attorney fees.

How can I reduce my legal fees?

Stay organized, respond promptly, be candid about the facts, and ask whether an uncontested or negotiated resolution is possible.

What can be expunged in Louisiana?

Many arrests and some convictions can be expunged after applicable waiting periods; eligibility depends on the offense and your record. Ask for a free eligibility review.

What family law matters do you handle?

Divorce, child custody, child support, adoption, emancipation, and division of community property.

How do you handle traffic citations?

Many citations can be resolved without a court appearance. Call the office with your ticket details.

Still have questions? Every case is different. Request a consultation and get answers specific to your situation.
