About Our Law Office

Providing legal services to Bossier City, Shreveport, and Northwest Louisiana since 2012.

Meet Your Attorney

Christopher M. Stahl

Christopher M. Stahl was born in Shreveport, Louisiana and raised in Minden. A North Louisiana native, he has built his practice on straightforward advice, responsive communication, and dedicated representation for clients across the Shreveport–Bossier area.

He graduated from Cedar Creek High School in Ruston, earned a Bachelor of Business Administration from Millsaps College in Jackson, Mississippi (2008), and received his Juris Doctorate from Loyola New Orleans College of Law in 2012, where he was honored with the Successions Law Excellence Award.

Admitted to Practice in Louisiana

Christopher was admitted to practice law in Louisiana in 2012 and is licensed before all state courts of Louisiana and the United States Federal Court for the Western District of Louisiana.

Community & Memberships

Christopher is a member of the Louisiana Bar Association, the Shreveport Bar Association, the Bossier Bar Association, and the Webster Bar Association. He is also a member of the Bossier Chamber of Commerce and the Krewe of Justinian, and a proud sponsor of the Parkway Football Team.
