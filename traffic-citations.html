Traffic Ticket Attorney in Bossier City & Shreveport

Protect your driving record, your insurance rates, and your CDL after a Louisiana traffic citation.

A traffic ticket can feel like a minor annoyance, but simply paying it is the same as pleading guilty — and that decision can cost you far more than the fine. If you have been cited in Bossier City, Shreveport, or anywhere in Northwest Louisiana, it is worth a quick conversation with an attorney before you pay. The Law Office of Christopher M. Stahl helps drivers protect their records and often resolves citations without the client ever having to appear in court.

Why Fight a Traffic Ticket?

Paying the ticket may seem like the fastest way to move on, but a guilty plea becomes part of your driving record and can trigger consequences that last far longer than the ticket itself. Contesting the citation gives you a chance to keep the violation off your record, avoid an insurance increase, or have the charge reduced to something that carries fewer consequences. Common citations we handle include:

  • Speeding and reckless or careless operation
  • Running a red light or stop sign
  • Improper lane usage and following too closely
  • Driving without proof of insurance
  • Driving under suspension and other moving violations

Tickets, Your Record, and Insurance

The real cost of a traffic ticket is usually not the fine — it is the long-term effect on your insurance. Convictions for moving violations stay on your record, and insurers routinely raise premiums for drivers who accumulate them. A single ticket can lead to higher rates for years, often costing far more than the citation. For drivers who already have violations on their record, an additional conviction can push rates even higher or put a license at risk. Resolving a ticket so that it does not appear as a conviction can protect both your record and your wallet.

Special Concerns for CDL Holders

If you hold a commercial driver’s license, a traffic ticket is a much bigger deal. CDL drivers are held to a higher standard, and certain “serious” violations can lead to disqualification of your commercial privileges — even when the ticket was received in your personal vehicle. Because your livelihood may depend on keeping your CDL, it is especially important not to simply pay a ticket without understanding how it could affect your commercial driving status. An attorney can work to resolve the citation in a way that protects your CDL whenever possible.

How the Office Often Resolves Citations

In many traffic matters, you can hire an attorney to appear and handle the citation on your behalf, so you do not have to take time off work or go to court yourself. Chris is familiar with the local courts and prosecutors in the Bossier City and Shreveport area and can often negotiate to have a charge dismissed or reduced — for example, to a non-moving violation that does not affect your record or insurance the way the original charge would. Every case is different, and the outcome depends on the specific citation, your driving history, and the court involved, but the goal is always to minimize the impact on your record and your daily life.

Don't Just Pay — Know Your Options

Before you mail in that payment, take a moment to understand what the citation could really cost you over time. A short call can help you decide whether it makes sense to fight the ticket, and in many cases the office can handle the entire matter for you with little disruption to your schedule.
