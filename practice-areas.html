Practice Areas

Focused, personal representation across the legal matters that affect Louisiana families most — from criminal charges and serious injuries to divorce, successions, and estate planning.

Family Law

Deciding to pursue custody, divorce, emancipation, or separation of property is often difficult given the emotional demands involved. Chris helps guide you through these situations with a plan tailored to your needs — and also handles adoptions.

Learn More →

Criminal Defense

If you or a loved one has been charged with an offense, you need to know your rights. Chris has handled hundreds of misdemeanor and felony matters across North Louisiana — including drug offenses, theft, DUI, and traffic citations.

Learn More →

Personal Injury

An injury is taxing physically and emotionally. Insurance adjusters work for their employers — not you. If you’ve been hurt in a fall, a car accident, or through another’s fault, Chris can help. Injury consultations are free with no up-front costs.

Learn More →

Expungements

Everyone makes mistakes — but a mistake doesn’t have to follow you for life. If a past issue is making it hard to find work, or you simply want a clean slate, an expungement may help. Contact Chris for a brief, free eligibility consultation.

Learn More →

Successions

Too often people put estate decisions on the back burner — which can lead to family infighting and added court costs. A succession doesn’t have to be difficult; many are uncontested and can be handled for a flat fee, quickly and painlessly.

Learn More →

Estate Planning

Protect your loved ones with a clear plan. Chris helps families put wills, trusts, and powers of attorney in place so your wishes are honored and your family is spared unnecessary cost and conflict.

Learn More →

Civil Disputes

Disputes come in many forms — with a neighbor, a business partner, or from a transaction. The goal is always to resolve the problem without litigation where possible, often through demand letters or a few calls, in the most cost-effective way.
